Questions

How long does approval take?

Most businesses hear back within a day. You'll be able to sign in and check status any time while you wait.

Can more than one person run the business account?

Yes — add co-owners by email or phone, and they get full access the moment they sign in.

Does Dayshift work on a phone?

Yes, both as a website and as a native app, with crew location tracking that keeps working in the background during a shift.

What kind of businesses is this for?

Built first for cleaning companies, and works just as well for lawn care, handyman, pet care, and similar crew-based service businesses.
