Questions

How long does approval take?

Most businesses hear back within a day. You can sign in and check status any time while you wait.

Can more than one person run the business account?

Yes. Add co-owners by email or phone, and they get full access the moment they sign in.

Does Dayshift work on a phone?

Yes. It works in any phone browser today, and a native Android app for crews is on the way, with iOS after that. Crew members share location only while they're on shift.

What kind of businesses is this for?

Built first for cleaning companies, and just as useful for lawn care, handyman, pet care and similar crew-based service businesses.
